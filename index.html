02 / About

I got into infrastructure through web development. At my agency I led migrations between WordPress and Shopify in both directions, and the headless builds pulled me toward servers. Some of those sites drew heavy traffic, so we had to plan an architecture that could scale.

This was before AI tools, so I learned servers through a lot of late-night reading. I ended up running a shared server for smaller client sites and setting up separate VPS instances for clients that needed isolation.

I closed the agency in 2026 to study cloud and network engineering. My goal is DevOps, because it draws on both my years with developers and my time on servers.

Managing developers taught me to give clear priorities and honest status. I stay on a problem until it's solved, and I tell you when I don't know something.

Outside of work, I lift and track my numbers.
